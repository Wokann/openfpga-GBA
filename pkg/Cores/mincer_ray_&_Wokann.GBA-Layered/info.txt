GBA-Layered

Original Pocket port by mincer_ray, based on GBA_MiSTer by its contributors.
Wokann: AI-generated cartridge hardware passthrough and cartridge save reading/writing features.

SD ROMs can use GBA cartridge hardware or a standard DS Rumble Pak, independently of cartridge saves. EZ rumble commands are translated to GPIO3. See LAYERED.md for supported protocols and setup.

Report Layered issues to github.com/Wokann/openfpga-GBA, not the upstream MiSTer project.
